Dear Anmol, 

I genuinely don't know if I have the right words for this.

First of all, I'm so glad you didn't scroll past that reel 😭 Because somewhere between one random reel and one curious click, you found Kutumb and somehow, you became a part of the little family I've been building with so much love.

And now you've given me something I'll treasure.

Your words about Raghav and Idhi especially touched a very personal place in my heart. You didn't just read their story—you understood the emotions underneath it. The silence, the misunderstandings, the patience, the quiet ways people love without always knowing how to say it. And the fact that you could see yourself somewhere within them... that means more to me than any number, ranking, or milestone ever could.

That line about knowing you're breaking a little every day and still choosing to get up and give your best... 🥹
I don't think you realize how deeply that stayed with me.

Sometimes, as an author, I wonder whether the emotions I pour into fictional people will actually reach someone on the other side of the screen. Your letter answered that question for me.

And Anmol... your name means precious, doesn't it?
I think it suits you rather beautifully🤍

Because this letter is precious to me.

Thank you for finding my story when you weren't even looking for one. Thank you for giving Kutumb a chance. Thank you for noticing the little things I tried to hide between chapters. Thank you for loving Raghav and Idhi, but more importantly, thank you for understanding them.

And please don't ever worry about whether your review reached me. Even if you had written it a hundred times, I'd still be grateful that you wanted to make sure I received it 🥹❤️

Readers often think authors give them stories.

But sometimes, readers unknowingly give authors something much bigger... a reason to believe that the stories they're writing are worth continuing.

You gave me that today.

So, from one reader-loving author to one very precious Anmol...

Thank you. Truly🫂🤍

I hope life gives you the same tenderness, patience, and love that you saw in Kutumb. I hope you keep finding stories that feel like home, and I hope somewhere along the way, my stories continue finding their way back to you too.

Keep reading. Keep dreaming. Keep being the beautiful human who took a chance on one random reel. 😂❤️

And whenever writing gets difficult, I'll remember that somewhere out there, Anmol didn't scroll past.

With all my love and gratitude,
ThatBrownCraft 🤎
