ThatBrownCraft

A little corner for stories, characters, and everything in between.

ThatBrownCraft is the official author website and creative space for ThatBrownCraft, bringing books, characters, stories, reader interactions, bonus content, and special little corners of the fictional worlds together in one place.

It is more than a traditional author website. It is a home for stories, characters, reader conversations, creative contributions, and the things that happen around the pages.

✦ What You'll Find

📚 Bookshelf

Explore the books and stories by ThatBrownCraft in one place.

Each book can include:

- Book information
- Series details
- Synopsis
- Publication information
- Reading and purchase links
- Featured status
- Related content
- Additional story material where available

The Bookshelf is the main place to discover the author's published and ongoing works.

🪶 Character Vault

Meet the characters behind the stories.

The Character Vault brings together character profiles and story-world details, allowing readers to explore the people who make each fictional universe what it is.

Character entries may include:

💌 Crafties

The Crafties section is the reader interaction space.

Readers can share their thoughts, reactions, reviews, creative work, and messages through the available features.

Depending on the section, readers may be able to submit:

- Reviews
- Postcards
- Fan art
- Story reactions
- Messages
- Other creative contributions

The idea is simple: readers aren't just reading from the sidelines. They get a little corner of the world too.

📮 Crafties Post Office

The Crafties Post Office is a special correspondence corner created for the little pieces of love that travel between the author and readers.

Readers can send postcards and messages through the Post Office, sharing their thoughts, wishes, reactions, questions, or anything they would like to leave behind.

And unlike a regular contact form, the Post Office is meant to feel like an actual exchange.

Postcards sent by Crafties can receive letters back from the author, creating a small collection of conversations between the pages and the people reading them.

The Post Office may feature:

- Reader postcards
- Messages from Crafties
- Author replies
- Letters written in response to reader postcards
- Special correspondence
- Reader wishes and story-related thoughts

It is essentially a tiny digital mailbox where readers can leave a postcard and, when selected for a response, find a letter waiting for them in return.

Because some messages deserve more than a quick reply in a comment section. 🤎

✦ The Stories

ThatBrownCraft focuses on character-driven fiction, emotional relationships, family dynamics, romance, and the small details that make fictional worlds feel lived in.

The website is designed to grow alongside the stories.

As new characters, books, chapters, bonus scenes, artwork, reader contributions, and special projects are added, the website grows with them.

✦ About ThatBrownCraft

ThatBrownCraft is an independent creative space built around storytelling and the communities that grow around it.

The website brings together fiction, fictional universes, characters, reader interactions, bonus content, and additional creative projects under one roof.

For readers, it is a place to discover stories.

For returning readers, it is a place to revisit familiar characters.

For Crafties, it is a place to leave a little piece of themselves behind.

And sometimes, hopefully, find a letter waiting for them too.
